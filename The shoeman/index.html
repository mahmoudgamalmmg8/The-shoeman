About THE SHOEMAN

Since 1888, THE SHOEMAN has been the premier purveyor of extraordinary footwear for discerning clients with... unusual tastes. Our collection consists of rare and haunted shoes, each with its own unique history and supernatural properties.

Every pair in our inventory has been carefully authenticated by our team of paranormal investigators and cobblers. We guarantee that each shoe comes with at least one verified supernatural attribute or your money back.

Our founder, Elias Shoeman, began this business after discovering a pair of boots that allowed him to walk through walls. Since then, we've specialized in sourcing the most remarkable footwear from around the world.
